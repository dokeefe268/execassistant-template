## E-500 notes
- It's a 2005 camera. Noise gets heavy above ISO 400, so learn to use light, aperture, and shutter speed before raising ISO.
- Shoot RAW so Lightroom has room for color grading.

## 8-week path to manual

Each Thursday session (after workout and meal prep): about 30 minutes shooting, 30 minutes editing.

| Weeks | Mode | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Aperture priority (A) | Depth of field: blurry backgrounds vs everything sharp |
| 3-4 | Shutter priority (S) | Motion: freeze vs blur, slowest handheld speed |
| 5-6 | Manual (M), ISO fixed at 100-400 | Read the exposure meter, balance all three settings |
| 7-8 | Manual in hard light | Golden hour, indoors, night, high contrast |

Daily: one iPhone photo practicing composition (rule of thirds, leading lines, framing).

## Posting rhythm (2-3 a week)
- Shoot on Saturday outings, hikes, and Thursday sessions
- Batch-edit on Thursday, and schedule the week's posts in Instagram
- Keep a few edited photos in reserve for busy weeks
